Privacy Policy

Border Builder - Last updated 28 May 2026

Border Builder does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

What Border Builder does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No network requests to our servers (we do not operate any).
  • No data leaves your device.

What Border Builder does do

  • The plans you create are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. They are accessible only to you.
  • Apple's App Store and TestFlight may collect crash reports and aggregate usage statistics independently of Border Builder. Apple's practices are governed by their own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Border Builder is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and note material changes in the app's release notes.

Contact

For privacy questions, open the App Store listing for Border Builder and tap App Support.
