Plants for partial shade

Plants for an east-facing border or dappled light.

An east-facing border or dappled light beneath open-canopy trees offers the gentlest growing conditions: morning sun, then shelter from the fierce midday glare. Many woodland-edge plants are at their best here, flowering well without scorching. The one hazard is the early sun striking frosted buds on a cold spring morning, so avoid the most frost-tender flowers in an east-facing spot. Keep the soil moisture-retentive with compost, since dappled sites can still dry under a tree canopy. Combine bold foliage with airy flowers to make the most of the moving light. These plants suit that in-between aspect, content with a few hours of sun and shade the rest of the day.
