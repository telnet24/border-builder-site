Foundation planting plants

Plants suited to a foundation planting planting scheme.

Foundation planting frames the front of a house, softening the join between building and garden and giving a tidy, settled look year round. Because it is on view in every season and at close range, it leans heavily on evergreens and reliable, low-maintenance shrubs that hold their shape without constant attention. Keep taller plants clear of windows and allow for mature spread so you are not forever cutting back from the walls. Repeat a few well-chosen plants for a calm, cohesive frontage rather than a one-of-everything collection. Vary leaf texture and add seasonal flower for relief. These plants are chosen for dependable structure and good manners close to the house, the kind that look composed every month of the year.
