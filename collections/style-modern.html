Modern plants

Plants suited to a modern planting scheme.

A modern border is built on restraint, structure and repetition rather than the floral abundance of a cottage scheme. A limited plant palette, repeated in bold drifts, gives the calm, intentional look; grasses and architectural foliage carry the design, with flower used as a controlled accent rather than the main event. Strong, simple shapes and a quiet colour range, often greens, whites and a single accent, do the work. The mistake is too many different plants, which reads as busy and undermines the clean effect. Pay attention to form, line and the spaces between plants. These plants are chosen for their architectural shape, fine texture and the disciplined repetition that defines a contemporary planting.
