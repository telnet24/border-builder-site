California Brittlebush

Encelia californica

California Brittlebush is a shrub for mid-border, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or sandy soil, flowering Mar, Apr, May, Jun.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 9a to 10b and rated H3 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
