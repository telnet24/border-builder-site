Sundrops

Oenothera fruticosa

Sundrops is a perennial for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering May, Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 45 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 9a, so check it suits your area before planting.
