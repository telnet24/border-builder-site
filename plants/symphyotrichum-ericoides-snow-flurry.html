Heath Aster 'Snow Flurry'

Symphyotrichum ericoides 'Snow Flurry'

Heath Aster 'Snow Flurry' is a perennial for edging, groundcover, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering Sep, Oct.

TypePerennial
Mature height15 cm
Spread60 cm
Aspectfull sun
FoliageDeciduous
HardinessUSDA 3a-8b
Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 15 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8b, so check it suits your area before planting.
