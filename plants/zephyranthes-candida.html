Rain Lily

Zephyranthes candida

Rain Lily is a bulb for bulb layer, edging, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or moist ground, flowering Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 25 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 20 cm of room to spread, roughly 5 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10a, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • It copes with ground that stays wet, which most border plants will not.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
