Dwarf Canadian hemlock

Tsuga canadensis 'Jeddeloh'

Dwarf Canadian hemlock is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, sandy soil or acid soil, flowering varies by climate.

TypeShrub
Mature height90 cm
Spread120 cm
Aspectfull sun to partial shade
FoliageEvergreen
HardinessUSDA 3a-7b, RHS H6
Pollinator-friendlyNo
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 7b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of shade, sandy soil, acid soil, evergreen plants, pet-safe plants.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
