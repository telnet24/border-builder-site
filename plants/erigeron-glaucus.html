Seaside Daisy

Erigeron glaucus

Seaside Daisy is a perennial for edging, groundcover, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or sandy soil, flowering Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug.

TypePerennial
Mature height30 cm
Spread60 cm
Aspectfull sun to partial shade
FoliageDeciduous
HardinessUSDA 7a-10b
Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 30 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
