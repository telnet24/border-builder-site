Slender deutzia 'Nikko'

Deutzia gracilis 'Nikko'

Slender deutzia 'Nikko' is a shrub for focal point, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or chalk, flowering May, Jun.

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 8b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
