Star Tickseed

Coreopsis pubescens

Star Tickseed is a perennial for mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

TypePerennial

Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 9b, so check it suits your area before planting.
