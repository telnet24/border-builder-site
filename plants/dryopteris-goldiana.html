Goldie's Wood Fern

Dryopteris goldiana

Goldie's Wood Fern is a perennial for mid-border, suited to shade and partial shade and clay, loam, sandy soil, acid soil or moist ground, flowering varies by climate.

TypePerennial
Mature height120 cm
Spread150 cm
Aspectshade and partial shade
FoliageDeciduous
HardinessUSDA 3a-8b, RHS H7
Pollinator-friendlyNo
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 150 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
