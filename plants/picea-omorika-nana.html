Dwarf Serbian Spruce

Picea omorika 'Nana'

Dwarf Serbian Spruce is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or sandy soil, flowering varies by climate.

Where to use it in a border

At around 150 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 7b, so check it suits your area before planting.
