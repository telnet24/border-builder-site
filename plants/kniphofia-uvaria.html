Red-hot Poker

Kniphofia uvaria

Red-hot Poker is a perennial for focal point, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

TypePerennial
Mature height120 cm
Spread60 cm

HardinessUSDA 5a-9b, RHS H6
Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
