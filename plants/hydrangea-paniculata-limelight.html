Hydrangea 'Limelight'

Hydrangea paniculata 'Limelight'

Hydrangea 'Limelight' is a shrub for focal point, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

TypeShrub

Pollinator-friendlyNo
Pet-safeNo

Where to use it in a border

At around 200 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 200 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8b and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
