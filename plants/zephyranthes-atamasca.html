Atamasco Rain Lily

Zephyranthes atamasca

Atamasco Rain Lily is a bulb for bulb layer, edging, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Mar, Apr, May.

Where to use it in a border

At around 25 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 15 cm of room to spread, roughly 7 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
