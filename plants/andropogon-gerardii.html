Big Bluestem

Andropogon gerardii

Big Bluestem is a grass for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and clay, loam or sandy soil, flowering Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 180 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 9a, so check it suits your area before planting.
