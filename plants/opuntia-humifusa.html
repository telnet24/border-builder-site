Eastern Prickly Pear

Opuntia humifusa

Eastern Prickly Pear is a perennial for focal point, groundcover, suited to full sun and sandy soil, flowering Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 30 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 9b, so check it suits your area before planting.
