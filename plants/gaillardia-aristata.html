Blanketflower

Gaillardia aristata

Blanketflower is a perennial for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

TypePerennial
Mature height60 cm
Spread45 cm
Aspectfull sun to partial shade
FoliageDeciduous
HardinessUSDA 3a-9b, RHS H7
Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 9b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
