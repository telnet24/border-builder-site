Sedum 'Autumn Joy'

Hylotelephium 'Herbstfreude'

Sedum 'Autumn Joy' is a perennial for mid-border, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Aug, Sep, Oct.

TypePerennial
Mature height50 cm
Spread60 cm
Aspectfull sun
FoliageDeciduous
HardinessUSDA 3a-9b, RHS H7
Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 50 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 9b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
