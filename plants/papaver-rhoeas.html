Common poppy

Papaver rhoeas

Common poppy is a perennial for mid-border, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug.

TypePerennial
Mature height60 cm
Spread30 cm

Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeNo

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 30 cm of room to spread, roughly 3 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 9a and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
