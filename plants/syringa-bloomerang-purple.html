Reblooming Lilac 'Bloomerang Purple'

Syringa 'Bloomerang Purple'

Reblooming Lilac 'Bloomerang Purple' is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and clay or loam, flowering Apr, May, Aug, Sep.

Where to use it in a border

At around 150 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 180 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 7a, so check it suits your area before planting.
