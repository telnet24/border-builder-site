Small Scabious

Scabiosa columbaria

Small Scabious is a perennial for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Jul, Aug, Sep.

Where to use it in a border

At around 45 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 40 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9a and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
