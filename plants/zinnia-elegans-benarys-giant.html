Zinnia 'Benary's Giant'

Zinnia elegans 'Benary's Giant'

Zinnia 'Benary's Giant' is an annual for focal point, mid-border, suited to full sun and loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 2a to 11b, so check it suits your area before planting.
