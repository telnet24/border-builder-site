Winterberry 'Berry Poppins'

Ilex verticillata 'Berry Poppins'

Winterberry 'Berry Poppins' is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam, acid soil or moist ground, flowering May, Jun.

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 9b, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of full sun, clay soil, acid soil, wet soil, pollinator-friendly plants.

Worth knowing

  • It copes with ground that stays wet, which most border plants will not.
  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
