Missouri Evening Primrose

Oenothera macrocarpa

Missouri Evening Primrose is a perennial for edging, groundcover, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering May, Jun, Jul, Aug.

TypePerennial

Spread45 cm

Where to use it in a border

At around 25 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 8b, so check it suits your area before planting.
