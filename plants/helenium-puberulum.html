Rosilla

Helenium puberulum

Rosilla is a perennial for mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 6a to 10a and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
