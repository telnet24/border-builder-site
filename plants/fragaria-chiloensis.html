Beach Strawberry

Fragaria chiloensis

Beach Strawberry is a perennial for edging, groundcover, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or sandy soil, flowering Mar, Apr, May, Jun.

TypePerennial
Mature height15 cm
Spread60 cm

Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 15 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
