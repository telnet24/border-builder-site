Hosta

Hosta 'Francee'

Hosta is a perennial for groundcover, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or moist ground, flowering Jul, Aug.

TypePerennial
Mature height50 cm
Spread90 cm
Aspectfull sun to partial shade
FoliageDeciduous

Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeNo

Where to use it in a border

At around 50 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of shade, clay soil, wet soil, pollinator-friendly plants.

Worth knowing

  • It copes with ground that stays wet, which most border plants will not.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
