Meadow Rue 'Black Stockings'

Thalictrum aquilegiifolium 'Black Stockings'

Meadow Rue 'Black Stockings' is a perennial for focal point, mid-border, growing to about 120 cm. It suits full sun to partial shade and flowers Jun, Jul.
