Dwarf Coyote Brush 'Pigeon Point'

Baccharis pilularis 'Pigeon Point'

Dwarf Coyote Brush 'Pigeon Point' is a shrub for groundcover, structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering Sep, Oct, Nov.

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 180 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
