Salvia 'Hot Lips'

Salvia microphylla 'Hot Lips'

Salvia 'Hot Lips' is a perennial for mid-border, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10b and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
