Snowy Mespilus

Amelanchier lamarckii

Snowy Mespilus is a shrub for structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, sandy soil or acid soil, flowering Apr.

Where to use it in a border

At around 500 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 400 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 8b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
