Umbrella Bamboo (clumping)

Fargesia murielae

Umbrella Bamboo (clumping) is a grass for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering varies by climate.

TypeGrass

Where to use it in a border

At around 300 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 150 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9a and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
