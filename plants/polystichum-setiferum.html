Soft Shield Fern

Polystichum setiferum

Soft Shield Fern is a perennial for focal point, mid-border, suited to shade and partial shade and clay, loam or acid soil, flowering varies by climate.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 8b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
