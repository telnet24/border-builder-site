Giant Sacaton

Sporobolus wrightii

Giant Sacaton is a grass for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering Aug, Sep, Oct.

TypeGrass
Mature height180 cm
Spread120 cm
Aspectfull sun

HardinessUSDA 5a-10b
Pollinator-friendlyNo
Pet-safeNot confirmed

Where to use it in a border

At around 180 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
