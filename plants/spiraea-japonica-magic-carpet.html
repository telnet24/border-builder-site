Spirea 'Magic Carpet'

Spiraea japonica 'Magic Carpet'

Spirea 'Magic Carpet' is a shrub for edging, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug.

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 8b, so check it suits your area before planting.
