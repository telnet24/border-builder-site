Panicle Hydrangea 'Quick Fire'

Hydrangea paniculata 'Quick Fire'

Panicle Hydrangea 'Quick Fire' is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

TypeShrub

FoliageDeciduous
HardinessUSDA 3a-8a
Pollinator-friendlyNo
Pet-safeNo

Where to use it in a border

At around 180 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 180 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8a, so check it suits your area before planting.
