Blue corydalis 'Blue Panda'

Corydalis flexuosa 'Blue Panda'

Blue corydalis 'Blue Panda' is a perennial for edging, groundcover, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or acid soil, flowering Apr, May, Jun.

Where to use it in a border

At around 30 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 30 cm of room to spread, roughly 3 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 8b and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
