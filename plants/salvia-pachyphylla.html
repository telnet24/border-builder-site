Mojave Sage

Salvia pachyphylla

Mojave Sage is a shrub for mid-border, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Mar, Apr, May, Jun.

Where to use it in a border

At around 75 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 10b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
