Maiden Grass 'Gracillimus'

Miscanthus sinensis 'Gracillimus'

Maiden Grass 'Gracillimus' is a grass for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and clay or loam, flowering Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 180 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9a and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
