Gulf Stream Heavenly Bamboo

Nandina domestica 'Gulf Stream'

Gulf Stream Heavenly Bamboo is a shrub for mid-border, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or sandy soil, flowering varies by climate.

Where to use it in a border

At around 110 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 6b to 9b and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
