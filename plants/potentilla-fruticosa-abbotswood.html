Shrubby Cinquefoil 'Abbotswood'

Potentilla fruticosa 'Abbotswood'

Shrubby Cinquefoil 'Abbotswood' is a shrub for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

TypeShrub

Where to use it in a border

At around 75 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 7b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
