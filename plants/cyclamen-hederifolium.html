Hardy Cyclamen

Cyclamen hederifolium

Hardy Cyclamen is a bulb for bulb layer, edging, groundcover, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, chalk or acid soil, flowering Sep, Oct, Nov.

Where to use it in a border

At around 15 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 20 cm of room to spread, roughly 5 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9a and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
