Late Aster 'October Skies'

Symphyotrichum oblongifolium 'October Skies'

Late Aster 'October Skies' is a perennial for groundcover, mid-border, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Sep, Oct.

TypePerennial
Mature height60 cm
Spread90 cm
Aspectfull sun
FoliageDeciduous
HardinessUSDA 3a-8b, RHS H6
Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
