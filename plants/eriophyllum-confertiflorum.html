Golden Yarrow

Eriophyllum confertiflorum

Golden Yarrow is a perennial for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

TypePerennial

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 10b and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
