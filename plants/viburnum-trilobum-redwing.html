American Cranberrybush 'Redwing'

Viburnum trilobum 'Redwing'

American Cranberrybush 'Redwing' is a shrub for structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or moist ground, flowering May, Jun.

Where to use it in a border

At around 240 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 240 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 2a to 7b, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of full sun, clay soil, wet soil, pollinator-friendly plants.

Worth knowing

  • It copes with ground that stays wet, which most border plants will not.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
