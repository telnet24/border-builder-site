Yew 'Densiformis'

Taxus x media 'Densiformis'

Yew 'Densiformis' is a shrub for structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering varies by climate.

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 180 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 7a, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
