Twinspur

Diascia personata

Twinspur is a perennial for mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or sandy soil, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

TypePerennial
Mature height90 cm
Spread60 cm

Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeNot confirmed

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7b to 9b and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
