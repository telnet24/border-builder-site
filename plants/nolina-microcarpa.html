Bear Grass

Nolina microcarpa

Bear Grass is a grass for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering May, Jun.

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 150 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 6a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
