Gerbera Daisy

Gerbera jamesonii

Gerbera Daisy is a perennial for edging, focal point, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug.

Where to use it in a border

At around 30 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 30 cm of room to spread, roughly 3 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 11b, so check it suits your area before planting.
