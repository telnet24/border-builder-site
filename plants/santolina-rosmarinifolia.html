Green lavender cotton

Santolina rosmarinifolia

Green lavender cotton is a shrub for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Jul, Aug.

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 75 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 6a to 9a and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
