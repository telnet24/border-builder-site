Violet Silverleaf

Leucophyllum candidum 'Thunder Cloud'

Violet Silverleaf is a shrub for mid-border, structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Sep.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 10b and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
