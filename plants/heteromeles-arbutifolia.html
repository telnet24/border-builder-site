Toyon

Heteromeles arbutifolia

Toyon is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or sandy soil, flowering Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 300 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 300 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of full sun, sandy soil, evergreen plants, pollinator-friendly plants.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
