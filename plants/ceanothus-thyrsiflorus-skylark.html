California Lilac 'Skylark'

Ceanothus thyrsiflorus 'Skylark'

California Lilac 'Skylark' is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering Apr, May, Jun.

Where to use it in a border

At around 150 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 180 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10a, so check it suits your area before planting.
