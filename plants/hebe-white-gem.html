Hebe 'White Gem'

Hebe 'White Gem'

Hebe 'White Gem' is a shrub for edging, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug.

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 75 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10a and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
