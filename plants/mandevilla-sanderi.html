Mandevilla

Mandevilla sanderi

Mandevilla is a climber for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 300 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 9a to 11a, so check it suits your area before planting.
