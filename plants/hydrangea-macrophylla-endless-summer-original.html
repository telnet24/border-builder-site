Bigleaf Hydrangea 'Endless Summer'

Hydrangea macrophylla 'Endless Summer Original'

Bigleaf Hydrangea 'Endless Summer' is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or acid soil, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

TypeShrub

Pet-safeNo

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 9b, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of shade, clay soil, acid soil.

Flowering through the year

JFMAMJJASOND

Worth knowing

  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
