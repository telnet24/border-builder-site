True Myrtle

Myrtus communis

True Myrtle is a shrub for structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug.

Where to use it in a border

At around 250 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 250 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 11b, so check it suits your area before planting.
