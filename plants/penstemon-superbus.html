Superb Penstemon

Penstemon superbus

Superb Penstemon is a perennial for focal point, mid-border, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Sep.

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10b and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
