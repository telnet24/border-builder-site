Blue Fescue

Festuca glauca 'Elijah Blue'

Blue Fescue is a grass for edging, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 25 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 25 cm of room to spread, roughly 4 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 8b and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
