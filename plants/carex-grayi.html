Gray's Sedge

Carex grayi

Gray's Sedge is a grass for edging, groundcover, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or moist ground, flowering May, Jun, Jul.

TypeGrass

Spread45 cm

Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 9b, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of shade, clay soil, wet soil, pet-safe plants.

Worth knowing

  • It copes with ground that stays wet, which most border plants will not.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
