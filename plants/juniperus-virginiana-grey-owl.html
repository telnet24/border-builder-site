Red Cedar 'Grey Owl'

Juniperus virginiana 'Grey Owl'

Red Cedar 'Grey Owl' is a shrub for structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering varies by climate.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 180 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 9b, so check it suits your area before planting.
