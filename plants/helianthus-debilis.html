Beach Sunflower

Helianthus debilis

Beach Sunflower is a perennial for edging, groundcover, suited to full sun and sandy soil, flowering May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

TypePerennial
Mature height45 cm
Spread90 cm
Aspectfull sun

HardinessUSDA 8a-10b
Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 45 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
