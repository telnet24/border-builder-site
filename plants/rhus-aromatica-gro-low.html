Fragrant Sumac 'Gro-Low'

Rhus aromatica 'Gro-Low'

Fragrant Sumac 'Gro-Low' is a shrub for groundcover, structure, suited to full sun and clay, loam or sandy soil, flowering Apr, May.

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 180 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 9a, so check it suits your area before planting.
