Spineless Prickly Pear

Opuntia ellisiana

Spineless Prickly Pear is a perennial for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and sandy soil, flowering May, Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
