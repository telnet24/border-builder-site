French marigold

Tagetes patula

French marigold is an annual for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun and clay, loam or sandy soil, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 30 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 30 cm of room to spread, roughly 3 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 2a to 11b and rated H2 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
