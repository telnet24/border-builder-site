Bottlebrush 'Little John'

Callistemon viminalis 'Little John'

Bottlebrush 'Little John' is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering Apr, May, Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 150 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 11b, so check it suits your area before planting.
