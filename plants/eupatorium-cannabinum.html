Hemp Agrimony

Eupatorium cannabinum

Hemp Agrimony is a perennial for mid-border, structure, suited to full sun and clay, loam or moist ground, flowering Jul, Aug, Sep.

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9a and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of full sun, clay soil, wet soil, pollinator-friendly plants.

Worth knowing

  • It copes with ground that stays wet, which most border plants will not.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
