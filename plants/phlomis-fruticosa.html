Jerusalem sage

Phlomis fruticosa

Jerusalem sage is a shrub for focal point, mid-border, structure, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug.

Where to use it in a border

At around 100 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 10b and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
