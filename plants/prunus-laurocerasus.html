Cherry Laurel

Prunus laurocerasus

Cherry Laurel is a shrub for structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Apr, May.

Where to use it in a border

At around 400 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 400 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 6a to 9a and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
