Italian Aster 'Rudolph Goethe'

Aster amellus 'Rudolph Goethe'

Italian Aster 'Rudolph Goethe' is a perennial for mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 8b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
