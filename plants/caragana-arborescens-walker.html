Weeping pea shrub

Caragana arborescens 'Walker'

Weeping pea shrub is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or sandy soil, flowering Apr, May.

Where to use it in a border

At around 180 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 2a to 7b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
