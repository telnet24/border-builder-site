Montbretia

Crocosmia 'George Davidson'

Montbretia is a perennial for bulb layer, mid-border, suited to full sun and clay, loam or sandy soil, flowering Jul, Aug.

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 30 cm of room to spread, roughly 3 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 6a to 9a and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
