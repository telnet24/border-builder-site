Saltbush

Atriplex halimus

Saltbush is a shrub for structure, suited to full sun and sandy soil or chalk, flowering Jul, Aug, Sep.

TypeShrub

Pet-safeNot confirmed

Where to use it in a border

At around 200 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 200 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7b to 10b and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
