Hebe 'Great Orme'

Hebe 'Great Orme' is a shrub for mid-border, structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10a and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
