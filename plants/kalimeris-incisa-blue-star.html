Kalimeris 'Blue Star'

Kalimeris incisa 'Blue Star'

Kalimeris 'Blue Star' is a perennial for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or chalk, flowering Jul, Aug, Sep.

TypePerennial
Mature height60 cm
Spread60 cm

FoliageDeciduous

Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeNot confirmed

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
