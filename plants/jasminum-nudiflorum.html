Winter Jasmine

Jasminum nudiflorum

Winter Jasmine is a climber for structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, flowering Jan, Feb, Mar.

TypeClimber

Where to use it in a border

At around 300 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 180 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 6a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
