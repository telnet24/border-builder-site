Pine Muhly

Muhlenbergia dubia

Pine Muhly is a grass for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 75 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 75 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 6a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
