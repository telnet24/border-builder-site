Blueberry

Vaccinium corymbosum

Blueberry is a shrub for mid-border, structure, suited to full sun and sandy soil or acid soil, flowering Apr, May.

Where to use it in a border

At around 150 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
