Big Muhly

Muhlenbergia lindheimeri

Big Muhly is a grass for mid-border, structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering varies by climate.

Where to use it in a border

At around 150 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10b and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
