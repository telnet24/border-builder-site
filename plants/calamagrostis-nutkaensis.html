Pacific Reedgrass

Calamagrostis nutkaensis

Pacific Reedgrass is a grass for mid-border, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or sandy soil, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug.

Where to use it in a border

At around 120 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 9b, so check it suits your area before planting.
