Bronze New Zealand Sedge

Carex comans 'Bronze Form'

Bronze New Zealand Sedge is a grass for edging, groundcover, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or sandy soil, flowering varies by climate.

Where to use it in a border

At around 35 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 9b and rated H4 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
