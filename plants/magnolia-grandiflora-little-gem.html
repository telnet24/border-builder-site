Southern Magnolia 'Little Gem'

Magnolia grandiflora 'Little Gem'

Southern Magnolia 'Little Gem' is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or acid soil, flowering May, Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 600 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 300 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 9b, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
