Panicle Hydrangea 'Bobo'

Hydrangea paniculata 'Bobo'

Panicle Hydrangea 'Bobo' is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 120 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8a, so check it suits your area before planting.
