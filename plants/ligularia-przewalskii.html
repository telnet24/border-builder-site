Ligularia

Ligularia przewalskii

Ligularia is a perennial for focal point, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or moist ground, flowering Jul, Aug.

TypePerennial

Where to use it in a border

At around 150 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 8b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • It copes with ground that stays wet, which most border plants will not.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
