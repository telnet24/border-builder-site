Bishop's flower

Ammi majus

Bishop's flower is an annual for mid-border, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

Where to use it in a border

At around 100 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 6b to 9b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
