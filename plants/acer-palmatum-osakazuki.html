Japanese Maple (autumn)

Acer palmatum 'Osakazuki'

Japanese Maple (autumn) is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or acid soil, flowering varies by climate.

Where to use it in a border

At around 450 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 400 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 6a to 8b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
