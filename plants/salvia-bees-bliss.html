Bee's Bliss Sage

Salvia 'Bee's Bliss'

Bee's Bliss Sage is a perennial for groundcover, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or sandy soil, flowering Apr, May, Jun.

Where to use it in a border

At around 25 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 240 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
