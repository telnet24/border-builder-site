Clematis 'Etoile Violette'

Clematis viticella 'Etoile Violette'

Clematis 'Etoile Violette' is a climber for structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or chalk, flowering Jul, Aug, Sep.

Where to use it in a border

At around 300 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 150 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 9a and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
