Paperbush

Edgeworthia chrysantha

Paperbush is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, flowering Jan, Feb, Mar.

Where to use it in a border

At around 150 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 150 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
