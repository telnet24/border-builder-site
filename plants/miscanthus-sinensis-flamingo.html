Silver Grass 'Flamingo'

Miscanthus sinensis 'Flamingo'

Silver Grass 'Flamingo' is a grass for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and clay or loam, flowering Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 160 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 100 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9a and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
