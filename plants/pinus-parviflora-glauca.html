Japanese White Pine

Pinus parviflora 'Glauca'

Japanese White Pine is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun and loam or sandy soil, flowering varies by climate.

Where to use it in a border

At around 300 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 250 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 8b and rated H6 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
