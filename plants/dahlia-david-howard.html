Dahlia 'David Howard'

Dahlia 'David Howard' is a bulb for focal point, mid-border, suited to full sun and clay or loam, flowering Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 100 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 11b and rated H3 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
