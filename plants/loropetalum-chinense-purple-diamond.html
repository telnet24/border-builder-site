Loropetalum 'Purple Diamond'

Loropetalum chinense 'Purple Diamond'

Loropetalum 'Purple Diamond' is a shrub for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or acid soil, flowering Mar, Apr, May.

Where to use it in a border

At around 150 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 150 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 7a to 10a, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
