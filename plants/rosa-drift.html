Drift Rose

Rosa 'Drift'

Drift Rose is a shrub for edging, groundcover, suited to full sun and clay or loam, flowering May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

TypeShrub
Mature height45 cm
Spread90 cm
Aspectfull sun

HardinessUSDA 5a-10a
Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 45 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 10a, so check it suits your area before planting.
