American Wisteria 'Amethyst Falls'

Wisteria frutescens 'Amethyst Falls'

American Wisteria 'Amethyst Falls' is a climber for focal point, structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering May, Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 600 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 300 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9a, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
