Perennial Cornflower 'Amethyst in Snow'

Centaurea montana 'Amethyst in Snow'

Perennial Cornflower 'Amethyst in Snow' is a perennial for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering May, Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 45 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 50 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
