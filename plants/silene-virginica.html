Fire Pink

Silene virginica

Fire Pink is a perennial for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam, flowering Apr, May, Jun.

TypePerennial

Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 40 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 25 cm of room to spread, roughly 4 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 8b, so check it suits your area before planting.
