Turtlehead

Chelone obliqua

Turtlehead is a perennial for mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or moist ground, flowering Aug, Sep, Oct.

TypePerennial
Mature height90 cm
Spread60 cm

Pollinator-friendlyYes
Pet-safeNot confirmed

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 60 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 5a to 9b and rated H5 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of clay soil, wet soil, pollinator-friendly plants.

Worth knowing

  • It copes with ground that stays wet, which most border plants will not.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
