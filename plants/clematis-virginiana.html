Virgin's Bower

Clematis virginiana

Virgin's Bower is a climber for structure, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay or loam, flowering Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 450 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 180 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 3a to 8a, so check it suits your area before planting.

Worth knowing

  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
