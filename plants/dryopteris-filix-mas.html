Male Fern

Dryopteris filix-mas

Male Fern is a perennial for focal point, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and clay, loam or acid soil, flowering varies by climate.

TypePerennial
Mature height90 cm

Pollinator-friendlyNo
Pet-safeYes

Where to use it in a border

At around 90 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 90 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 8b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of shade, clay soil, acid soil, evergreen plants, pet-safe plants.

Worth knowing

  • Evergreen, so it keeps the border furnished through winter.
  • It tolerates acid soil but dislikes shallow chalk.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
