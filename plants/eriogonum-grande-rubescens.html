Red Buckwheat

Eriogonum grande rubescens

Red Buckwheat is a perennial for edging, mid-border, suited to full sun to partial shade and loam or sandy soil, flowering Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct.

Where to use it in a border

At around 30 cm, it sits best along the front edge, where a low, spreading habit reads best. Give it about 45 cm of room to spread, roughly 2 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 8a to 10b, so check it suits your area before planting.
