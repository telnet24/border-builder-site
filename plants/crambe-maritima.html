Sea Kale

Crambe maritima

Sea Kale is a perennial for focal point, mid-border, suited to full sun and loam, sandy soil or chalk, flowering May, Jun, Jul.

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best toward the back of a border, or on its own as a focal point with room around it. Give it about 75 cm of room to spread. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 8b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.
