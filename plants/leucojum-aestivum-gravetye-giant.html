Summer Snowflake

Leucojum aestivum 'Gravetye Giant'

Where to use it in a border

At around 60 cm, it sits best through the middle of a border, between taller structure behind and edging in front. Give it about 15 cm of room to spread, roughly 7 to the metre when planted in a drift. It is hardy across USDA zones 4a to 8b and rated H7 by the RHS, so check it suits your area before planting.

It appears in our lists of shade, clay soil, wet soil, pollinator-friendly plants.

Worth knowing

  • It copes with ground that stays wet, which most border plants will not.
  • It tolerates a shaded, north-facing spot.
  • Not recorded as pet-safe; site it away from pets that graze if that matters to you.
  • Its flowers are valued by bees and other pollinators.
